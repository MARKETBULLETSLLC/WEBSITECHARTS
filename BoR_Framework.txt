OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Box-o-Rox™ (BoR™) is a wheat marketing decision framework used to guide grain
sale timing for subscribers holding physical bushels or managing price risk.

BoR is technically-informed but NOT purely technical. While status signals are
derived from technical indicators, all final status changes are instituted by
Gary Hofer. This human confirmation layer exists to account for overarching
fundamental narratives that a mechanical system cannot fully process.

BoR is a trading system with human governance — not an autonomous algorithm.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STATUS: SUSPEND
  Definition: Hold all tranches. No sales are recommended at this time.
  Implication: Pricing conditions do not favor execution. Accumulated sale
               tranches remain suspended and continue to accumulate.
  Subscriber Action: Do not sell. Monitor Signal Proximity for approaching
                     status change.

STATUS: EXECUTE
  Definition: Sell all suspended tranches. This is a full accumulated position
              liquidation — not a partial or staged sale.
  Implication: Pricing conditions have met the threshold for action. All
               tranches accumulated during the Suspend period are now sold.
  Subscriber Action: Execute sales across all suspended tranches.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SIGNAL PROXIMITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Signal Proximity is an integrated threshold indicator embedded within BoR.
It measures how close current pricing is to triggering a potential status
change (Suspend → Execute, or Execute → Suspend).

Signal Proximity does NOT change BoR status on its own. It informs Gary's
assessment and is published to subscribers as an early-warning gauge.

STATUS CHANGE PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Technical indicator(s) reach threshold (Signal Proximity: At Threshold)
2. Gary reviews fundamental overlay — supply/demand, basis, macro context
3. Gary institutes status change (Suspend → Execute or Execute → Suspend)
4. New status published to subscribers in pre-dawn commentary
5. Signal Proximity resets relative to new status baseline

NOTE: Gary is the sole authority on BoR status changes. No automated system
      or third party may institute a status change.
