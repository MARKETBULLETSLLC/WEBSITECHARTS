The Warm Signal (TWS)
Daily · Updated each session
Box-o-Rox · Live Benchmark
One benchmark, posted before the open.
Checking current status…

Box-o-Rox measures Chicago SRW settlement against two lines, the Suspend/Execute line and the Tripwire, and holds one of two states: SUSPEND or EXECUTE. Each state is a marketing posture, not a price target.

  • For producersA plain cue for when to price the next tranche, so the marketing plan runs on schedule instead of on headlines.
  • For lenders, elevators & input dealersA shared yardstick for the conversation across the desk. Everyone at the table works from the same read.

It doesn't need to be complicated. It needs to be honest about where the market sits in its cycle. Box-o-Rox informs; the marketing plan decides.
