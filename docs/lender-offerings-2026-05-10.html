Collateral Risk Briefing

Commentary Insert — Drop into Daily Analysis
LENDER NOTE: If your borrower holds unpriced grain today, the collateral under that operating loan just moved. July SRW touched $6.50/bu on April 28 — a nearly two-year high — before retreating below $6.00 on improving Plains weather forecasts. That's a 50-cent swing in twelve trading sessions. A 10,000-bushel position that looked like $65,000 in collateral two weeks ago now appraises closer to $59,000. Not a crisis — but a number worth recalculating before the May 12 WASDE lands.

Rule of thumb for lenders: Size operating loans against the October–December average, not the spring peak. Producers who priced in spring look fully collateralized in April; borrowers who held into July can gap 15–20% below origination value. Review collateral any time the market moves more than 40 cents in a 30-day window.
