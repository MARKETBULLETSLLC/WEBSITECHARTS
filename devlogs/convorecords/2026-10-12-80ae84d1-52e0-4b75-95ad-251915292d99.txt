There are two good open-source options for Android. Which one fits depends mostly on your phone's chip.

## 1. Local Dream: best for generating images on the phone itself
- **What it is:** a free, open-source Android app that runs Stable Diffusion on the phone with no internet and no account. It does text-to-image, image-to-image and inpainting (repainting part of an image), and you can load your own SD1.5 models.
- **Hardware:** it's fastest on **Snapdragon** phones, because it uses the Qualcomm neural chip (NPU). SD1.5 models run on most Snapdragon 8-series chips from the last few years. The higher-quality SDXL models need a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 or newer. Other phones (Samsung Exynos, MediaTek, Pixel) fall back to the CPU or GPU, which works but is much slower.
- **Where to get it:** GitHub (xororz/local-dream) or Google Play.

## 2. SDAI (Stable Diffusion AI): the most flexible
- **What it is:** an open-source app (AGPL-3.0, no ads, no tracking) that's on **F-Droid**, the open-source app store, as well as Play and GitHub.
- **How it works:** it can generate on the phone, but its main strength is connecting to other services:
  - **AI Horde:** a free network of volunteers' computers that generates images for you. Good for a weaker phone, but there can be a wait in the queue.
  - **Your own PC:** if your Windows machine has a decent NVIDIA graphics card, you can run Stable Diffusion (AUTOMATIC1111 or SwarmUI) on it and use the phone as the remote control. This gives the best quality: a PC can run much larger models than any phone.
  - It also connects to paid services (OpenAI, Stability) if you ever want them.

## My recommendation
- **Recent Snapdragon flagship:** start with **Local Dream**.
- **Any other phone, or you want better image quality:** use **SDAI**, with AI Horde first and your own PC as the upgrade.

## One caveat
The apps are FOSS, but the image models mostly are not. Stable Diffusion models are "open weights" under licenses that allow commercial use with some restrictions. Read the license of any model you use for MarketBullets marketing.

A marketing note: AI images are fine for filler graphics and social media backgrounds. Keep the boots photo and anything presented as "our farm" or "our people" real, though. Your audience can spot generated farm imagery, and authenticity is part of what you're selling.

He should pick **"Connect to Horde AI cloud"**. It's free, marked Stable, and doesn't care what chip his phone has.

**Next steps:**
1. Tap **Connect to Horde AI cloud**, then **Next**.
2. The setup screen will ask for an **API key**:
   - **Quick start:** use the anonymous or default key (all zeros, `0000000000`). It works right away, but anonymous users go to the back of the queue, so waits are longer.
   - **Better:** register at **aihorde.net** for a free personal key (no payment) and paste it in. He'll get faster service, and it improves the more he uses it.
3. In the generation screen, he can choose among the models the volunteers are running. The more popular ones come back faster.

**What to skip:**

| Provider | Why skip it |
|---|---|
| OpenAI, Stability AI, Fal.ai, ArliAI | Paid services that need a card and an account |
| Automatic 1111, Swarm UI | Only work with Stable Diffusion running on his own PC |
| Hugging Face | Free tier exists but needs an account and token, and has tight limits. Fine as a backup later |
| Local Diffusion Microsoft ONNX | Older option (last updated 2024) and slow |
| Local Diffusion SDXL | Runs offline on the phone, but it's marked Alpha and SDXL is heavy. On a 6GB phone, expect very slow images or crashes. Only worth a try for fun once Horde is working |

If Horde is too slow for him, Local Dream from my earlier message is still worth trying as the offline option.
