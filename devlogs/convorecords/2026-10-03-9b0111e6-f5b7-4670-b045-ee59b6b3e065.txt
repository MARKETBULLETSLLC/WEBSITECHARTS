- **One standard format.** The script would find any of the styles above and replace it with a single house style, e.g. `Thursday Oct 1, 2026: CLOSE`. Every chart would then match without you retyping anything.
- **Built into the existing auto-commit watcher.** It already sees each export land in `charts/`. It would stamp the date line first, then commit, so there are no extra steps for you.
- **It skips rather than guesses.** If it can't read the date line clearly, it leaves the chart alone and logs it for you to check. A few lines that sit over grid lines read as garbled, so this will happen on some charts.
- **A "frozen" list.** Expired spreads like SPD_*_N_U and U_Z should keep their final date. I'd add a column to the master chart index CSV (Auto or Frozen) to control this.
